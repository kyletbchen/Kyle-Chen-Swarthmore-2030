Personal Project

Eagle Scout Project

Woodworking Scamatics Leadership

Overview

An ESP32-based device that monitors ethylene gas concentration to track and accelerate fruit ripening, combining a gas sensor, microcontroller firmware, and an enclosed ripening chamber.

I identified an issue faced by the staff at my local park, Locust Grove: community members had trouble finding certain trails. I set out to address this by leading a project to install colorful gates at two trailheads. After fundraising over $1,000+ from my local community, I used computer-aided design software to create schematics and organized a team of 15 Scouts to bring the vision to life. We drilled holes, painted wood, and installed the gates, making the park more accessible and welcoming.

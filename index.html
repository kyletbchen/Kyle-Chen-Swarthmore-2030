Background

I'm an engineering student at Swarthmore College with a focus on hardware and embedded systems — a pull that started in high school and has shown up in every project since, from ultrasonic sensing rigs to plasma physics experiments in the garage.

On campus, I'm a Powertrain Engineer and the Head of Sponsorship for Swarthmore's Formula SAE team, splitting my time between drivetrain design work and building the sponsor relationships that fund the car. Outside of coursework and the team, I'm an Eagle Scout, which is where a lot of my project-planning and leadership habits started.
